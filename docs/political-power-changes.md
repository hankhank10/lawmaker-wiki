# Political Power changes: 3 October 2026

!!! note "This page is temporary"
    It's here to explain today's rebalance and will be removed after a while. The permanent numbers live in the [Quick Reference](reference.md#political-power-pp), and each rule is explained on its own page.

Political Power (PP) costs had grown up one feature at a time, and some had drifted out of line with each other. We went through every cost and adjusted the ones that no longer made sense. **Income is unchanged** (100 PP to start, +1 PP per game day, 120 PP cap, and the same bonuses for holding power), and so are most prices.

## What changed

| Action | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Change a pillar](gameplay/parties.md#changing-a-pillar) | 75 PP | **50 PP** |
| [Form a government](gameplay/cabinet.md) | 30 PP | **20 PP** (still 10 PP when every post is vacant) |
| [Ban a party from public events](gameplay/executive-actions.md) | 30 PP | **40 PP** |
| [Order an arrest](gameplay/executive-actions.md#order-arrest) | 30 PP | **40 PP** |
| [Lift a public events ban](gameplay/executive-actions.md) | 30 PP | **15 PP** |
| [Issue a pardon](gameplay/executive-actions.md) | 30 PP | **15 PP** |
| [Replace a candidate who can no longer stand](gameplay/elections.md#candidacies) | 20 PP | **Free** |
| [Apply to join a bloc](gameplay/communication.md#international-blocs) | 20 PP, never refunded | 20 PP, **refunded if the bloc's leaders deny you** |
| [Recruit an activist](gameplay/characters.md#recruiting-activists) | 10 PP flat | **Escalates with roster size**: see below |

## Why

- **Pillars (75 → 50).** Changing a pillar only affects your own party, yet it cost more than a *major* constitutional change (60 PP) and the same as calling a whole constitutional convention. It's still one of the most expensive things a party can do, just no longer out of proportion.
- **Government formation (30 → 20).** Coalition talks often fail or expire, and each attempt is paid for up front. Since forming a government is something every party does after every election, the price of a failed attempt was too steep. Replacing a sitting government is still a real commitment.
- **Executive actions (30 → 40 / 15).** Banning a rival's campaigning or arresting their people is far more powerful than most 30 PP actions, so it now costs more. Undoing that harm is deliberately cheap, so a new government that wants to free a banned party or pardon someone can do it without a big PP bill. Autocracy points are unchanged.
- **Replacing an invalid candidate (20 → free).** If your candidate is arrested, expelled, retires or is barred by the Supreme Court, you didn't choose to lose them. Previously an arrest also cost the victim 20 PP to replace the candidate. Swapping a candidate who is still able to stand costs 20 PP as before.
- **Bloc applications.** The 20 PP is there to stop application spam, not to punish you for a leader's decision. If you're denied you get it back; if you're accepted it's spent, as before.
- **Recruiting.** The price of an activist now climbs with the size of your roster (this went in a few days ago, and the manual still described a flat 10 PP). It's now documented: **10 PP while you have fewer than 10 activists, then your roster size + 1** (the 11th activist costs 11 PP, the 21st costs 21 PP, the 31st costs 31 PP). This is deliberate: you don't need 30 activists, and a big roster should cost you. Expelling an activist lowers the price of the next recruit.

## What didn't change

- **Constitutional changes and conventions.** Major changes are meant to be unaffordable without a [constitutional convention](gameplay/constitution.md#constitutional-conventions). A package of three major changes costs 180 PP, more than the 120 PP cap, so it can only be opened during a convention, where a package costs at most 75 PP. That's the point of the convention.
- **How fast PP is earned.** Parties that hold power (head of state, cabinet posts, seats, a majority) keep earning PP faster, and the bonuses still compound. Holding power is meant to snowball.
- **Laws, budgets, early elections and no-confidence votes** stay at 30 PP, Supreme Court appeals at 60 PP, founding a bloc at 50 PP, and calling a convention at 75 PP.

If you're mid-way through saving PP for something on this list, your balance is untouched. Only the prices changed.
