# Avalon

Avalon is the flagship country: a large Westminster-style **constitutional monarchy** of 65 million people, with a single 650-seat **Parliament** and the most established community. There's a lot of competition and plenty of room for coalition-building, and it's the recommended place to start.

- **Government:** a Prime Minister heads the government and holds the foreign-policy and treaty powers. The cabinet also has a Chancellor of the Exchequer (banks and monetary policy), Home Secretary (police, intelligence, martial law), Foreign Secretary, Defence Secretary and Lord Chancellor.
- **Parliament** holds the powers to regulate businesses, political parties and the environment.
- **The Crown of Avalon** is a hereditary monarchy, and holds the power to grant pardons.

When the throne is vacant, each active party may nominate one activist, and Avalon picks the nomination from the **oldest** party. The winning party goes dormant while it reigns. See [Hereditary Monarchy](../gameplay/monarchy.md) for the full rules and the price of the throne.
